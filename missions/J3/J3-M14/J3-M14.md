# Part A — Human-Readable Mission Document

# 1. Mission Metadata

**Mission ID:** J3-M14
**Journey:** Become an Investor
**Mission Title:** What Is Investing?
**Core Concept:** Investing means using money today to buy something that may generate income or increase in value in the future, while accepting the possibility of loss.
**Target Age Group:** Teens 13–17
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Estimated Duration:** 4 minutes
**Currency and Locale:** United Kingdom — GBP (£), en-GB
**Language:** English

**Learning Objective:**
Understand investing as using money today for the possibility of future growth while accepting uncertainty and risk.

**Primary Misconception:**
Investing and gambling are always the same.

**Secondary Misconceptions:**

* Every investment is guaranteed to make money.
* Giving money, lending money, and investing money are the same thing.

**Key Takeaway:**
Investing means accepting uncertainty for the possibility of future growth.

# 5. Main Interaction

**Interaction Type:** Decision Tree

**Interaction Title:** Gift, Loan, or Investment?

**Instruction:**
Explore all three paths. Follow what happens to your £300 and identify which choice is an investment.

## Starting Node

Alex asks for **£300** to buy a second grill for the burger shop.

You have three choices:

1. Give the money
2. Lend the money
3. Buy part of the business

## Educational Assumptions

* This is a fictional, adult-supported learning scenario.
* The £300 amounts are classroom examples.
* The business may succeed, struggle, or close.
* Research can improve understanding but cannot remove uncertainty.
* Any repayment or ownership arrangement would need clear terms.
* The activity does not recommend investing in a real business.
* Real investments may involve additional rules, costs, taxes, and legal requirements.

---

## Path 1 — Give the Money

**Choice:**
Give Alex £300 as a gift.

**Immediate Consequence:**
Alex can buy the grill. You help the business without asking for repayment, ownership, or a share of future profits.

### Follow-Up Question

What are you entitled to receive?

#### Options

1. **Nothing financially**
2. Guaranteed monthly profit
3. Ownership of the grill
4. Repayment with interest

**Recommended Answer:**
Nothing financially

**Explanation:**
A gift is given without expecting repayment, ownership, or financial growth.

### Terminal Outcome

**Helpful gift, not an investment**

The shop may succeed, but you do not automatically receive money or ownership because your £300 was a gift.

---

## Path 2 — Lend the Money

**Choice:**
Lend Alex £300 under an agreement to repay it.

**Immediate Consequence:**
Alex receives the money. You expect repayment according to the agreement, possibly with an agreed interest amount.

### Follow-Up Question

What is the main financial promise?

#### Options

1. **Alex should repay the agreed amount**
2. You own part of the shop
3. You receive every future profit
4. Repayment is impossible to miss

**Recommended Answer:**
Alex should repay the agreed amount

**Explanation:**
Lending creates a repayment claim, not ownership. Repayment may still be delayed or missed if the borrower cannot pay.

### Terminal Outcome

**Loan with repayment risk**

You do not own part of the shop. You expect repayment, but receiving every payment is not guaranteed.

---

## Path 3 — Buy Part of the Business

**Choice:**
Use £300 to buy a small ownership share in the burger shop.

**Immediate Consequence:**
You become a part-owner. Your result depends on how the business performs and on the ownership agreement.

### Follow-Up Question

What could happen to your money?

#### Options

1. **It could grow, stay similar, or fall**
2. It must double
3. Alex must repay £300 next month
4. It becomes a gift

**Recommended Answer:**
It could grow, stay similar, or fall

**Explanation:**
Ownership may produce income or increase in value if the shop succeeds. It may also lose value if the shop performs poorly.

### Terminal Outcome

**Investment with uncertain results**

You own part of something that may create real value, but there is no guaranteed profit or repayment date.

---

## Final Decision Node

**Question:**
Which path is the investment?

### Options

1. Give the money
2. Lend the money
3. **Buy part of the business**

**Correct Answer:**
Buy part of the business

**Explanation:**
Buying part of the shop creates ownership and the possibility of future growth or income, together with the risk of loss.

## Correct or Recommended Completion State

The learner explores all three paths and identifies:

* **Gift:** No repayment, ownership, or expected financial return
* **Loan:** Repayment agreement without business ownership
* **Investment:** Ownership with possible gains and possible losses

## Trade-Off Summary

### Giving

* Helps another person
* No repayment expected
* No ownership
* No expected profit

### Lending

* Creates a repayment agreement
* May include interest
* Does not create business ownership
* Repayment may be delayed or missed

### Investing

* Creates ownership
* May provide income or growth
* Does not guarantee repayment or profit
* Some or all of the money could be lost

## Investing and Gambling

Both investing and gambling involve uncertainty, but they are not always the same.

An investment can represent ownership or another financial claim connected to something that may create real value. It can be researched, although research cannot guarantee success.

Gambling usually involves staking money on an uncertain event without gaining ownership of a productive asset. Highly speculative investing can still resemble gambling when decisions rely mainly on chance or excitement.

## Validation Method

* Check each follow-up answer.
* Require the learner to explore all three paths.
* Complete the interaction when the learner correctly identifies the ownership path as investing.

## Success Feedback

Strong analysis. You separated a gift, a loan, and an investment—and recognised that ownership brings both opportunity and risk.

## Incorrect Feedback

Check what the person receives: nothing financial, a repayment promise, or ownership with uncertain results.

## Incomplete Feedback

Explore all three paths before identifying the investment.

## Retry Behaviour

Keep completed paths marked. Return only the incorrect follow-up question, show one short hint, and allow unlimited retries.

## Accessibility Alternative

Present the decision tree as three numbered, linear paths. Each path contains the choice, consequence, question, explanation, and return button.

**Screen-reader instruction:**
Choose Gift, Loan, or Ownership. Listen to the consequence, answer the follow-up question, and return to explore the remaining paths.

**Primary Button Label:** Finish the Decision

**Reveal Title:** Ownership With an Uncertain Future

**Explanation:**
You saw that giving, lending, and investing are different. **Investing** means using money to buy something that may produce income or rise in value later. Buying part of the burger shop created ownership, but no guaranteed profit. The shop could succeed, struggle, or close. Investing is not automatically gambling because an investment can be researched and linked to something creating value. However, both involve uncertainty, and speculative decisions can resemble gambling.

Investing offers possible growth, but your result is never guaranteed.

# 8. Quiz

## Question 1 — Recognition

**Question:**
Which situation is an investment?

### Options

1. **Buying 5% of a small business**
2. Giving a friend birthday money
3. Lending £20 with an agreement to repay it
4. Buying food from a shop

**Correct Answer:**
Buying 5% of a small business

**Correct-Answer Explanation:**
Buying 5% creates ownership. The owner may benefit if the business succeeds but may lose money if it struggles.

**Incorrect Feedback — Giving a friend birthday money:**
That is a gift because repayment or ownership is not expected.

**Incorrect Feedback — Lending £20 with an agreement to repay it:**
That is lending. It creates a repayment agreement rather than business ownership.

**Incorrect Feedback — Buying food from a shop:**
That is a normal purchase. You receive food rather than ownership in the shop.

**Misconception Being Tested:**
Giving, lending, purchasing, and investing are the same thing.

---

## Question 2 — Misconception

**Question:**
Which statement best compares investing and gambling?

### Options

1. **Both involve uncertainty, but investing can involve researched ownership in something that creates value**
2. They are always exactly the same
3. Every researched investment makes money
4. Gambling gives ownership of a business

**Correct Answer:**
Both involve uncertainty, but investing can involve researched ownership in something that creates value

**Correct-Answer Explanation:**
Investing may connect money to a productive business or asset. It still involves risk, and research cannot guarantee a profit.

**Incorrect Feedback — They are always exactly the same:**
Both involve uncertainty, but investments can provide ownership or claims linked to real assets and economic activity.

**Incorrect Feedback — Every researched investment makes money:**
Research can improve understanding, but investments can still lose value.

**Incorrect Feedback — Gambling gives ownership of a business:**
A wager normally does not provide ownership of a productive business or asset.

**Misconception Being Tested:**
Investing and gambling are always the same.

---

## Question 3 — Application

**Question:**
You buy a share of a small app business. Which outcome should you expect?

### Options

1. **The value may rise or fall depending on the business**
2. Your money must double
3. The owner must repay you next week
4. You cannot lose any money

**Correct Answer:**
The value may rise or fall depending on the business

**Correct-Answer Explanation:**
An ownership investment can benefit from business success, but poor results can reduce its value or cause a loss.

**Incorrect Feedback — Your money must double:**
No investment is guaranteed to double or produce any profit.

**Incorrect Feedback — The owner must repay you next week:**
Ownership is different from a loan. It normally has no fixed repayment date.

**Incorrect Feedback — You cannot lose any money:**
Investment value can fall, and some or all of the invested money may be lost.

**Misconception Being Tested:**
Every investment is guaranteed to make money.

## Concepts Being Assessed

- **Build a Budget:** Cover needs, allow reasonable wants, and allocate limited income.
- **Emergency Fund:** Build and protect accessible emergency savings.
- **Good Debt and Harmful Debt:** Judge borrowing by purpose, affordability, and repayment pressure.
- **Spot Financial Scams:** Recognise pressure, secrecy, guarantees, and unsafe requests.
- **Set Financial Goals:** Create a measurable and affordable financial goal.
- **Long-Term Investing:** Invest only when ready, diversify, and respond thoughtfully to volatility.

## Learning Objective

Apply budgeting, saving, emergency planning, debt management, scam awareness, goal-setting, and investing concepts across one simulated financial year.

## Primary Combined Misconception

Long-term wealth comes mainly from one lucky investment rather than a series of consistent financial decisions.

## Secondary Misconceptions

- Investing should always come before building emergency savings.
- One financial mistake permanently ruins a person’s financial future.
- A higher investment balance automatically means stronger financial health.

## Key Takeaway

**Financial strength comes from consistent decisions, not one lucky investment.**

# Stage 3 — Handle a Surprise Expense

## Stage Metadata

**Concepts Assessed:** emergency_fund, debt_management, needs_versus_wants, budget_adjustment  
**Interaction:** Predict Outcome Decision Tree

## Situation

An essential appliance breaks. The repair costs Rs. 40,000. Earlier emergency, savings, wants, and debt choices determine the available responses.

## Learner Objective

Predict the intended resource and choose a complete repair plan.

## Interaction Configuration

**Prediction Question:** Which resource was designed for this kind of expense?
- Emergency savings **(Preferred)**
- Next month’s rent
- Borrowed investment money

**Prediction Explanation:** Emergency savings protect the normal budget from genuine surprises.

**Starting Decision:** How will you fund the repair?

## Available Choices

### Use the emergency fund

**Classification:** Recommended

**Reason:** Enough emergency money must exist.

**Immediate Financial Effect:** Up to Rs. 40,000 is deducted from emergency savings.

**Educational Feedback:** The fund performs its intended purpose.

**Short-Term Consequence:** The repair is completed without debt.

**Possible Later Consequence:** Emergency savings need rebuilding.

### Use general savings

**Classification:** Reasonable

**Reason:** Accessible savings can solve the problem.

**Immediate Financial Effect:** General savings fall by Rs. 40,000.

**Educational Feedback:** The repair is covered, but another priority slows.

**Short-Term Consequence:** No debt is created.

**Possible Later Consequence:** Goal progress becomes harder.

### Borrow the full amount

**Classification:** Risky

**Reason:** Repayment creates future pressure.

**Immediate Financial Effect:** Debt rises by Rs. 40,000 and monthly repayment becomes Rs. 4,000.

**Educational Feedback:** Borrowing may be necessary when savings are insufficient.

**Short-Term Consequence:** The appliance is repaired.

**Possible Later Consequence:** Future goal and investment capacity fall.

### Skip an essential payment

**Classification:** Risky

**Reason:** An essential obligation remains uncovered.

**Immediate Financial Effect:** Missed obligations increase by Rs. 40,000.

**Educational Feedback:** This moves the problem rather than solving it.

**Short-Term Consequence:** Another need becomes overdue.

**Possible Later Consequence:** Investment readiness decreases.

### Combine savings and reduced wants

**Classification:** Reasonable

**Reason:** Several flexible sources share the cost.

**Immediate Financial Effect:** Emergency savings, optional spending, then general savings fund Rs. 40,000.

**Educational Feedback:** This works when the emergency fund is still small.

**Short-Term Consequence:** The repair is completed without new debt.

**Possible Later Consequence:** Savings fall and enjoyment is reduced.

## Recommended Decision Logic

**Generally Strongest:** Use sufficient emergency savings; otherwise combine accessible savings and flexible spending before avoidable debt.

**Other Reasonable Paths:**
- General savings may be used when emergency savings are small.
- Borrowing may be necessary when accessible resources are insufficient.

**Main Trade-Off:** Savings reduce future protection; debt preserves cash but creates repayment pressure.

# Stage 4 — Avoid a Financial Scam

## Stage Metadata

**Stage ID:** J5-FB30-ST4  
**Timeline:** Month 6  
**Concepts Assessed:** scam_awareness, verification, pressure_resistance  
**Interaction:** Sort Categories Decision Tree

## Situation

A message guarantees that Rs. 25,000 will become Rs. 50,000 next week and demands secrecy.

## Learner Objective

Identify warning signs and choose a safe or risky response.

## Interaction Configuration

| Item | Recommended Category |
| --- | --- |
| Guaranteed high return | Warning Sign |
| Pressure to act immediately | Warning Sign |
| Request for secrecy | Warning Sign |
| Professional-looking website | Not Proof of Safety |
| Referral rewards | Not Proof of Safety |
| Send money to a personal account | Warning Sign |
| Clear explanation of risks | Useful Verification |
| Independent registration information | Useful Verification |

**Ambiguity Explanation:** Referral rewards are not proof of fraud or safety.

**Starting Decision:** What do you do next?

**Message:** “Invest Rs. 25,000 today and receive Rs. 50,000 next week. Returns are guaranteed. Keep this private.”

## Available Choices

### Send Rs. 25,000 immediately

**Classification:** High Risk

**Reason:** Guarantees, urgency, and secrecy were accepted.

**Immediate Financial Effect:** Rs. 25,000 is lost in the fictional scam.

**Educational Feedback:** Professional design did not prove safety.

**Short-Term Consequence:** Savings fall.

**Possible Later Consequence:** Goal and emergency capacity weaken.

### Ask for more testimonials

**Classification:** Risky

**Reason:** The promoter controls the evidence.

**Immediate Financial Effect:** No money moves yet.

**Educational Feedback:** Testimonials may be selected or false.

**Short-Term Consequence:** Pressure continues.

**Possible Later Consequence:** Independent verification is still needed.

### Verify independently

**Classification:** Recommended

**Reason:** Information is checked outside the promoter’s control.

**Immediate Financial Effect:** No money is sent.

**Educational Feedback:** Independent checks are stronger than appearance.

**Short-Term Consequence:** Savings remain protected.

**Possible Later Consequence:** Later goals are unaffected by scam loss.

### Refuse to act under pressure

**Classification:** Recommended

**Reason:** Urgency is rejected.

**Immediate Financial Effect:** No money is sent.

**Educational Feedback:** A genuine opportunity should allow time for review.

**Short-Term Consequence:** Control remains with the learner.

**Possible Later Consequence:** The offer may be checked later or ignored.

### Share banking passwords

**Classification:** Invalid

**Reason:** Sensitive credentials cannot be used for verification.

**Immediate Financial Effect:** The action is blocked.

**Educational Feedback:** Never share passwords or security codes.

**Short-Term Consequence:** Credentials remain protected.

**Possible Later Consequence:** The learner must choose another response.

## Recommended Decision Logic

**Generally Strongest:** Refuse pressure and verify the company, payment destination, risks, and claims independently.

**Other Reasonable Paths:**
- Ignoring the message is reasonable when the learner does not wish to investigate.

**Main Trade-Off:** Verification takes time but protects savings and control.

## Updated State Screen

**Title:** Scam Decision Recorded

- **Scam Losses:** 0 → after_decision. Sending money creates a visible loss.
- **General Savings:** before_offer → after_offer. Savings remain protected unless money is sent.

**Learning Message:** Urgency, secrecy, and guarantees should trigger independent verification.

**Next-Stage Teaser:** Turn the Rs. 180,000 goal into a realistic plan.

## Retry Behaviour

Keep correct classifications. Credential sharing must be revised; other outcomes carry forward.

# Part A — Human-Readable Mission Document

# 1. Mission Metadata

**Mission ID:** J4-M22
**Journey:** Grow Wealth Wisely
**Mission Title:** Risk and Reward
**Core Concept:** Investments with greater potential returns often involve greater uncertainty and a wider range of possible gains and losses.
**Target Age Group:** Adults 18+
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Estimated Duration:** 4 minutes
**Currency and Locale:** United Kingdom — GBP (£), en-GB
**Language:** English

**Learning Objective:**
Understand that potential return and uncertainty are related.

**Primary Misconception:**
Higher risk always produces higher profit.

**Secondary Misconceptions:**

* A stable investment option can never lose purchasing power.
* A high-return opportunity must be a better investment.
* Risk only means how much profit an investment may produce.

**Key Takeaway:**
Higher risk may create greater potential returns, but never guarantees them.

# 5. Main Interaction

**Interaction Type:** Predict Outcome

**Interaction Title:** Choose the Most Likely Range

**Instruction:**
Review each fictional option. Predict the range of possible outcomes, then compare what could happen during favourable and difficult conditions.

## Educational Assumptions

* This is a fictional classroom comparison.
* Each option starts with £1,000.
* The time period is one year.
* No real company, fund, savings product, or return rate is represented.
* The outcome descriptions demonstrate uncertainty rather than predict actual performance.
* Fees, taxes, inflation, and currency changes are simplified or excluded unless stated.
* Lower price movement does not mean an option is completely risk-free.
* Greater uncertainty may create greater potential returns and greater potential losses.
* No option is presented as universally suitable or recommended.
* Past results and professional management cannot guarantee future outcomes.

---

## Option A — Stable Path

**Description:**
A fictional low-volatility option designed to change relatively little over one year.

**Typical Characteristics:**

* Narrower range of possible outcomes
* Lower potential for a large gain
* Lower likelihood of a large price fall
* Inflation may still reduce purchasing power

### Prediction Question

Which outcome range best fits the Stable Path?

#### Prediction Options

1. **Small gain, little change, or small loss**
2. Large gain or complete loss
3. Guaranteed profit
4. Exactly the same return every year

**Educational Outcome:**
Small gain, little change, or small loss

**Explanation:**
A stable option generally has a narrower range of possible results. It may reduce some uncertainty but cannot remove every risk.

---

## Option B — Moderate Path

**Description:**
A fictional mixed investment containing both steadier and more changeable holdings.

**Typical Characteristics:**

* Moderate range of possible outcomes

### Prediction Question

Which outcome range best fits the Moderate Path?

#### Prediction Options

1. No change is possible
2. **Moderate gain or moderate loss**
3. Guaranteed large profit

**Educational Outcome:**
Moderate gain or moderate loss

**Explanation:**
This option accepts more uncertainty for greater potential growth, but its result may still be positive, negative, or close to unchanged.

## Option C — High-Risk Path

**Description:**
A fictional investment in an early-stage company with an uncertain future.

**Typical Characteristics:**

* Wide range of possible outcomes
* Possibility of a large gain
* Possibility of a large loss
* The business may fail even if the idea appears promising

#### Prediction Options

2. Only a small change is possible
3. **Large gain, large loss, or another uncertain result**

**Educational Outcome:**
Large gain, large loss, or another uncertain result

**Explanation:**
High risk increases the range of possible outcomes. It may create greater upside, but it also increases the chance and size of potential losses.

# 6. Concept Reveal

**Reveal Title:** A Wider Range of Possibilities

**Explanation:**
You predicted a narrower outcome range for the stable option and a wider range for the high-risk option. **Investment risk** is the uncertainty that the actual result may differ from what you expect, including the possibility of loss. Investors may accept more risk because an investment offers greater potential returns. However, potential is not a promise. A high-risk investment may earn more, earn less, or lose money. Risk and possible reward are related, but higher risk never guarantees higher profit.

**Suggested Visual:**
Three outcome bands widen from Stable to Moderate to High Risk, extending towards both gains and losses.

**Explain It Simply:**
More potential reward usually means accepting more uncertainty and possible loss.

# 8. Quiz

## Question 1 — Recognition

**Question:**
Which option has the widest range of possible outcomes?

### Options

1. A low-volatility savings option
2. A mixed portfolio
3. **An investment in one untested start-up**
4. Cash kept for a short-term bill

**Correct Answer:**
An investment in one untested start-up

**Correct-Answer Explanation:**
The start-up could grow significantly, struggle, or fail, giving it a wider range of possible results.

**Incorrect Feedback — A low-volatility savings option:**
This would generally have a narrower range of possible outcomes than an untested business.

**Incorrect Feedback — A mixed portfolio:**
A mixed portfolio can still lose value, but spreading holdings may reduce dependence on one uncertain company.

**Incorrect Feedback — Cash kept for a short-term bill:**
Cash may face inflation and other risks, but its short-term price movement is generally narrower.

**Misconception Being Tested:**
All investment choices have the same level of uncertainty.

---

## Question 2 — Misconception

**Question:**
An investment is described as high risk. What does that guarantee?

### Options

1. A high profit
2. A complete loss
3. A better result than every safer option
4. **Nothing about the final profit**

**Correct Answer:**
Nothing about the final profit

**Correct-Answer Explanation:**
High risk describes greater uncertainty and loss potential. It does not guarantee a positive or negative result.

**Incorrect Feedback — A high profit:**
Higher potential return is not the same as a guaranteed return.

**Incorrect Feedback — A complete loss:**
A large loss may be possible, but it is not the only possible outcome.

**Incorrect Feedback — A better result than every safer option:**
A high-risk investment may perform better or worse than a lower-risk option.

**Misconception Being Tested:**
Higher risk always produces higher profit.

---

## Question 3 — Application

**Question:**
Two investments offer the same expected goal. One has much wider possible gains and losses. What should the investor recognise?

### Options

1. The wider range guarantees success
2. Both investments have identical risk
3. **The wider-range investment involves greater uncertainty**
4. The safer option cannot lose purchasing power

**Correct Answer:**
The wider-range investment involves greater uncertainty

**Correct-Answer Explanation:**
A wider range means the actual result could differ more significantly from what the investor expects.

**Incorrect Feedback — The wider range guarantees success:**
A wider range includes the possibility of larger losses as well as larger gains.

**Incorrect Feedback — Both investments have identical risk:**
The investment with the wider possible outcomes carries greater uncertainty.

**Incorrect Feedback — The safer option cannot lose purchasing power:**
Inflation and other risks can still affect options with relatively stable prices.

**Misconception Being Tested:**
Risk only describes possible reward rather than uncertainty and possible loss.

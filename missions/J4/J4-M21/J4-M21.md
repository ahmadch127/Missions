# Part A — Human-Readable Mission Document

# 1. Mission Metadata

**Mission ID:** J4-M21
**Journey:** Grow Wealth Wisely
**Mission Title:** Diversification
**Core Concept:** Diversification means spreading money across different investments and asset types so financial results do not depend too heavily on one company or outcome.
**Target Age Group:** Adults 18+
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Estimated Duration:** 4 minutes
**Currency and Locale:** Pakistan — PKR (Rs.), en-GB
**Language:** English

**Learning Objective:**
Understand how spreading money across different investments can reduce concentration risk without eliminating the possibility of loss.

**Primary Misconception:**
Diversification guarantees that an investor will not lose money.

**Secondary Misconceptions:**

* Owning several investments from the same company or industry provides complete diversification.
* Diversification always produces higher returns than concentrating money in one investment.

**Key Takeaway:**
Diversification reduces concentration risk but does not remove all risk.

Triggered when:

# 6. Concept Reveal

**Reveal Title:** Reduce Dependence, Not Every Risk

**Explanation:**
You saw how one company’s fall had a larger effect when all Rs. 10,000 depended on it. **Diversification** means spreading money across different investments and asset types so one result has less influence. Meaningful diversification also considers industries and risk sources, not only the number of holdings. It can reduce concentration risk, but markets, inflation, interest rates, currencies, or several investments falling together can still cause losses. Diversification manages risk; it does not guarantee safety or higher returns.

**Suggested Visual:**
One concentrated tower splits into several smaller towers, while a wider market-risk cloud remains above all of them.

**Explain It Simply:**
Spread exposure to reduce concentration, not to guarantee profit.

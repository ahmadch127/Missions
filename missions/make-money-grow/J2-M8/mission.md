# Part A — Human-Readable Mission Document

# 1. Mission Metadata

**Mission ID:** J2-M8
**Journey:** Make Money Grow
**Mission Title:** What Banks Do
**Core Concept:** Banks help people safely store, access, transfer, and keep track of their money.
**Target Age Group:** Kids 8–12
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Estimated Duration:** 4 minutes
**Currency and Locale:** United Kingdom — GBP (£), en-GB
**Language:** English

**Learning Objective:**
Understand the basic role of banks and how common banking tools help people manage their money.

**Primary Misconception:**
Keeping money in a bank automatically makes a person wealthy.

**Secondary Misconceptions:**

* A bank account is only a place where money is stored.
* Money kept at home is always as safe and convenient as money kept in a bank.

**Key Takeaway:**
Banks help store, move, and manage money.

# 5. Main Interaction

**Interaction Type:** Match Pairs

**Interaction Title:** Match the Banking Tools

**Instruction:**
Match each banking tool with the job it performs.

## Educational Scenario Assumptions

* Jamie uses the account with help from a trusted adult.
* The account already contains money.
* An ATM can provide cash when the account has enough money and the correct access details are used.
* A bank transfer moves money between accounts.
* A statement records money entering and leaving the account.
* Real accounts may have rules, limits, or fees that an adult should check.

## Left-Side Items

1. Bank account
2. ATM
3. Bank transfer
4. Statement

## Right-Side Items

1. Stores money
2. Accesses cash
3. Moves money
4. Records transactions

## Correct Pairs

### Bank Account → Stores Money

**Explanation:**
A bank account keeps Jamie’s money balance in one organised place.

### ATM → Accesses Cash

**Explanation:**
An ATM can let Jamie withdraw cash from money already available in the account.

### Bank Transfer → Moves Money

**Explanation:**
A transfer sends money from one bank account to another without handing over physical cash.

### Statement → Records Transactions

**Explanation:**
A statement shows money paid in, money spent or transferred, and the account balance.

## Placement Rules

* Each banking tool must match with one job.
* Each job can be used only once.
* There is one correct match for every tool.
* The order in which pairs are completed does not matter.

## Correct Completion State

* **Bank account:** Stores money
* **ATM:** Accesses cash
* **Bank transfer:** Moves money
* **Statement:** Records transactions

## Feedback for Incorrect Matching

### Bank Account Matched With Accesses Cash

The account holds the balance. An ATM is one tool that can help access cash from it.

### ATM Matched With Moves Money

An ATM mainly provides access to cash. A bank transfer moves money between accounts.

### Bank Transfer Matched With Records Transactions

A transfer moves money. The statement keeps a record of that movement.

### Statement Matched With Stores Money

A statement displays account activity. The account itself holds the money balance.

### General Incorrect Feedback

Look at the action word. Does the tool store, access, move, or record money?

## Validation Method

Check each pair when the learner submits it. Complete the activity when all four tools are correctly matched.

## Success Feedback

Perfect match! Banks use different tools to help people store, access, move, and track their money.

## Incorrect Feedback

That pair does not quite fit. Think about the main job performed by the banking tool.

## Incomplete Feedback

Match all four banking tools before finishing.

## Retry Behaviour

Keep correct pairs connected. Separate only the incorrect pair, show one short hint, and allow unlimited retries.

## Accessibility Alternative

Present each banking tool with a dropdown or radio-button list of the four possible jobs.

**Screen-reader instruction:**
Select a banking tool, choose its main job, and submit. Repeat until all four tools have a match.

**Primary Button Label:** Check My Matches

# 8. Quiz

## Question 1 — Recognition

**Question:**
Luca needs to send £8 to his cousin in another city. Which banking tool could move the money?

### Options

1. **Bank transfer**
2. ATM
3. Statement
4. Mattress

**Correct Answer:**
Bank transfer

**Correct-Answer Explanation:**
A bank transfer moves money from one account to another without requiring Luca to hand over physical cash.

**Incorrect Feedback — ATM:**
An ATM helps access cash. It does not usually send money directly to another person’s account.

**Incorrect Feedback — Statement:**
A statement records transactions. It does not perform the transfer itself.

**Incorrect Feedback — Mattress:**
Money under a mattress cannot be sent electronically to another account.

**Misconception Being Tested:**
A bank account is only a place where money is stored.

---

## Question 2 — Misconception

**Question:**
Priya deposits £50 into a bank account. Does the account automatically turn it into £100?

### Options

1. **No, the account does not automatically double the money**
2. Yes, every bank doubles deposits
3. Yes, because stored money creates wealth
4. Yes, after using an ATM

**Correct Answer:**
No, the account does not automatically double the money

**Correct-Answer Explanation:**
The account helps Priya store and manage her £50. Simply placing money in an account does not automatically make her wealthy.

**Incorrect Feedback — Yes, every bank doubles deposits:**
Banks do not automatically double deposited money.

**Incorrect Feedback — Yes, because stored money creates wealth:**
Storing money can help protect and organise it, but storage alone does not create new wealth.

**Incorrect Feedback — Yes, after using an ATM:**
An ATM lets Priya access money already in the account. It does not create additional money.

**Misconception Being Tested:**
Keeping money in a bank automatically makes a person wealthy.

---

## Question 3 — Application

**Question:**
Jay wants to check where his pocket money went last month. Which tool should he use?

### Options

1. **Statement**
2. ATM
3. Mattress
4. Bank transfer

**Correct Answer:**
Statement

**Correct-Answer Explanation:**
A statement records money entering and leaving the account, helping Jay review previous transactions.

**Incorrect Feedback — ATM:**
An ATM can help access cash, but it is not the main tool for reviewing every past transaction.

**Incorrect Feedback — Mattress:**
Cash stored at home does not automatically create a record of when and where it was spent.

**Incorrect Feedback — Bank transfer:**
A transfer moves money. A statement records that movement and other transactions.

**Misconception Being Tested:**
Money kept at home is always as safe and convenient as money kept in a bank.
